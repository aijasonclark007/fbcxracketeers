5. Standings

Type each game's score in the schedule above. Teams are ranked by match wins, then total points scored, then head-to-head. The top 2 of each bracket advance.

6. Knockout round

Single elimination: a team that loses is out. Semifinal losers share 3rd place.

The teams with the most wins and points score the best seeds. When the field is not 4, 8 or 16 teams, the top seeds get a BYE and go straight to the next round.
